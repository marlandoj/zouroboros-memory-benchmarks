# Arm C — reasoning and timeline levers for the three weak BEAM abilities (2026-11-23)

Commissioned by Marlando, 2026-11-23. Follow-up to the [past.dev parity decomposition](parity-2026-11-23.md).

Aggregate results only: no dataset rows, dialogue, or per-question answers are reproduced here, matching the parity report's classification.

## Background

The parity decomposition moved BEAM 100K from 54.42% (November) to 60.03% (Arm A, configuration parity) to 88.27% (Arm B, prompt/model/judge parity), but three abilities barely moved and hold most of the remaining gap:

| Ability | November | Arm A | Arm B |
|---|---|---|---|
| knowledge_update | 62.50% | 65.00% | 65.62% |
| multi_session_reasoning | 50.28% | 53.86% | 66.93% |
| contradiction_resolution | 48.12% | 44.38% | 69.69% |

Arms A and B used byte-identical evidence, so retrieval is not the bottleneck for these three — the reader fails to reconcile state, conflicts, and cross-session aggregation. Arm B ran with reasoning off (ExaBase parity). Arm C tests two reader-side levers on exactly those 120 questions (40 per ability) before anyone builds ingest-time machinery.

## Design (pre-registered; no mid-run tuning)

Fixed across all arms: the stored Arm B evidence replayed (no re-retrieval — identical evidence is what makes the comparison clean); ExaBase answer and judge prompts verbatim; reader and judge both `openai/gpt-5.6-luna` via OpenRouter (Arm B's route); ExaBase rubric judge with reasoning off, identical to Arm B.

- **C0 (variance control):** Arm B repeated unchanged — reader reasoning off (`reasoning: {effort: "none"}`), evidence presented byte-identically to Arm B. Every other delta is judged against the C0 spread, not against zero.
- **C1 (reasoning lever):** identical to C0 except reasoning **enabled** for the reader via OpenRouter `reasoning: {effort: "medium"}`. Judge unchanged.
- **C2 (timeline presentation lever):** reasoning off, prompts unchanged, but the evidence block re-presented as an explicit chronological timeline — items ordered by session and turn, each headed with its session date, session id, turn number, and role — built **only** from metadata already in the stored Arm B rows and the BEAM chat data. No new content, no summaries, no LLM-generated annotation.

C2 fidelity was verified per question, fail-closed: the pipeline's chunk assembly (round expansion, dedup, ability-aware chronological sort) was reconstructed from the stored metadata and chat data and had to reproduce the stored Arm B evidence byte-identically, or differ only by the ingest defense layer's `[REDACTED:*]` substitutions (in which case the stored, redacted text is what the timeline carries). **120/120 questions verified applicable** (110 byte-identical, 10 redaction-recovered); 0 questions C2-not-applicable.

## Results

120/120 questions scored in every arm. Zero errors, zero uncounted errors, zero judge exclusions in all arms.

| Ability | Arm B (stored) | C0 (Arm B re-run) | C1 (reasoning on) | C2 (timeline) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| contradiction_resolution | 69.69% | 68.75% | 70.62% | 70.00% |
| knowledge_update | 65.62% | 60.62% | 65.62% | 63.12% |
| multi_session_reasoning | 66.93% | 64.63% | 69.18% | 65.26% |
| **pooled (120)** | **67.41%** | **64.67%** | **68.48%** | **66.13%** |

**Noise band:** |C0 − Arm B| = **2.74pp** on the identical question set with identical evidence — this is pure model nondeterminism (and any judge nondeterminism) on this set.

## Verdict

- **C1 — reader reasoning (`effort: medium`): SHOWN.** +3.81pp pooled over C0, exceeding the 2.74pp nondeterminism band measured by re-running Arm B unchanged. Net +11 questions improved (23 up, 12 down), with gains in all three abilities. Enabling reader-side reasoning reconciles state, conflicts, and cross-session aggregation that the reasoning-off reader fumbles.
- **C2 — explicit chronological timeline presentation: NOT SHOWN.** +1.46pp pooled, inside the noise band. Presentation alone — ordering and date/session labels made explicit — does not reliably move the reader when reasoning is off. (Note: Arm B's evidence for these abilities was already chronologically sorted; C2 made the ordering *explicit and labelled* rather than implicit. That residual difference is within noise.)

**Implication for ingest-time work:** a reader-side reasoning lever recovers a meaningful share of the weak-ability gap at zero ingest cost. The timeline result gives no evidence that presentation structure is the binding constraint, so ingest-time machinery (supersession chains, conflict detection, entity resolution) should be justified against a reasoning-enabled reader baseline, not against the Arm B reasoning-off baseline.

## Scope and caveats

- One reasoning setting was tested (`effort: medium`), pre-registered; other efforts were not explored (no mid-run tuning).
- The noise band is a single re-run (C0 vs stored Arm B). +3.81pp clears it, but the band itself has sampling uncertainty; a second C0 replicate would sharpen it.
- 120 questions, three abilities, one reader/judge model route. No other abilities, no LoCoMo, no re-retrieval — by design.
- C2's timeline is built exclusively from stored metadata and chat data, with fail-closed verification; the 10 redaction-recovered questions carry the defense-redacted text exactly as the stored Arm B evidence did.
