## Transport recovery disclosure

The first direct-OpenAI LoCoMo attempt hit repeated account credit failures and was stopped. It is preserved outside the repository and excluded from recovery scores. Its recorded successful-response cost is **$27.4850**. Both exact model IDs then passed OpenRouter probes, so the scored LoCoMo Arm A was restarted from zero and its fresh 100-question smoke using `openai/gpt-6.1-sol` and `openai/gpt-4o`: identical prompts, retrieval, K, token cap, and model names. This is an API transport recovery, not a model or tuning change. The recovery smoke scored 90.00% with zero errors. No direct-API rows are mixed into the clean recovery scores.

Across the two interrupted direct attempts, 54 failure rows after retries are preserved and counted as operational errors. Their failed-call usage is unavailable; no dollar value is invented for it. A subsequent concurrency-30 OpenRouter burst hit the in-flight credit guard on 577 rows; those rows are also preserved and were retried at concurrency 10. Final scored rows have zero errors.
